01 — About NEO

Game Creators First.
Crafted To Be Felt.

At its core, NEO is a next-generation game development studio dedicated to crafting unforgettable worlds, visceral combat, and hauntingly atmospheric stories — led by our flagship dark-fantasy RPG, Crimson Night.

Alongside our core game studio, our bespoke NEO Hardware & Accessories division exists with a single purpose: to deliver the most immersive, tactile feeling possible. Every chair, headset, and precision instrument is tuned directly by our game designers so players don't merely watch the screen — they physically feel every blade clash, heartbeat, and atmospheric tremor as if standing inside the world itself.
