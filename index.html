Frequently Asked Questions

When will Crimson Night officially release? ✕
Crimson Night is scheduled for global release in Fall 2026 across PC (Steam, Epic Games), PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S. Closed Beta testing begins in Summer 2026 for all pre-order holders.
Who is the protagonist Kaelen and can I customize him? +
Kaelen is the canonical Ashen Lancer exiled from the Cathedral of Valdoria. While his core lore and spear combat stance form the heart of the campaign, players can fully customize armor sets, cloak pigments, socketed blood alchemy gems, and secondary weapons.
How does the Lovecraftian Bestiary impact gameplay? +
Entities like Malakor and The Ossuary Amalgam feature multi-phase cosmic encounters with sanity drain mechanics. Precision deflections and tactical stance switches with Kaelen's spear are required to shatter their eldritch poise.
How does the NEO hardware co-engineering benefit players? +
Through our direct partnership with NEO, the game features zero-latency input polling, certified 240Hz frametime stability, custom NEO RGB lighting integration synced to blood moon phases, and custom EQ presets for the NEO Pro Headset.
